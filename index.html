Saving your results as a PDF
Your browser's print dialog will open next. Follow these three steps to save a clean copy of your work:
  1. Set Destination to "Save as PDF" (instead of a printer).
  2. Open "More settings" and check the box for "Background graphics". This keeps the colored bars and numbers in your saved PDF.
  3. Click Save and choose where to save the file on your computer.
If you skip step 2, the bars in your PDF will appear blank or white. The chart needs background graphics turned on to show colors.
